## Visual decisions and revisions

The style is warm comic fantasy anime with small expressive merchants, distinct
town palettes and readable location silhouettes. Properties carry their own
scene; the same scene can supply a card crop and an arrival banner.

| Town | Reference and visual direction |
|---|---|
| Greenhorn Fields / brown | Konosuba: beginner village, cabbages and merchants |
| Overtime Hollow / light blue | Black Company: mines, overwork and gem cutting |
| Three Altars / purple | Konosuba, KamiKatsu, Lazy Dungeon Master: water, invented worship and naps |
| Wildmark / orange | Surviving the Game as a Barbarian: frontier provisions, hunting and fishing |
| Glimmerland / red | Amagi Brilliant Park: attractions, original mascots and comic maintenance |
| Curtain Call / yellow | Combatants Will Be Dispatched!: theater and indirect background references |
| Wolfcoin / green | Spice & Wolf: trade, harvest and hidden wolf details |
| Royal Ziggurat / dark blue | FGO Babylonia: monumental architecture and a hidden wealthy visitor |

ALICE on the puppet theater, AGENT N°6 on the pub, its small adult scientist
and the two distant ice/fire stage figures are requested decorative references.
They do not change game names or rules. Ukon is separately attributed below.

The first south-portal studies used trucks. They were replaced by wooden
merchant carts; the selected catalogue excludes those earlier studies.
Portals show travel between fantasy towns rather than arrival from a modern
world. Four variants on each route combine two origin and two destination
town contexts: south-west 5-15, west-north 15-25, north-east 25-35, east-south
35-5. These are 16 images for four routes, without extra destination choices.

There are six intended player appearances: human, elf and dwarf, each male or
female. Male dwarves retain a beard; female dwarves do not. Existing scene
workers and the recurring dwarf card protagonist are not assigned player seats.

Merchant emblems belong only in the property-card frame/header, as separate
owner stamps. Do not paint them into the original scenery or arrival banners.
The five choices have different shapes. Ownership still needs a readable label.

Ukon was adapted from a supplied appearance reference into a new welcoming
pose. The maintainer approved the mascot's appearance; this does not validate
its eventual layout. Character design belongs to kitsune_tsuki: retain the
[attribution and separate creator terms](mascot/ATTRIBUTION.md). No original
3D model, mesh or texture is included with this adaptation.

## Export and integration work, separate from generation

- Review crops for wide arrival banners, property cards and card faces. Keep
  ears, hands, central actions and hidden decorative details readable. Produce
  delivery sizes/compressed exports without replacing full-resolution masters.
- If required, prepare vector versions of logos and emblems. Current deliveries
  are raster PNGs; no SVG deliverable is claimed.
- Connect assets to server-filtered views and confirmed events. Names, prices,
  rents, levels, owners and outcomes remain interface text, not painted facts.
- Present one appropriate scene after confirmed movement, including chained
  moves. Support inspection, catch-up, reload and reduced motion without
  duplicating success scenes or blocking legal actions.
- Add merchant-emblem selection, persistence and owner mapping in property-card
  frames; do not assign an emblem to an unowned property.
- Use the same opaque neutral mask for every unauthorized retained-card view.
  Rope, mirror and retained curse must not leak through filenames, URLs, alt
  text, DOM, preloading or animation choices. A mask image alone does not prove
  runtime secrecy.
- Validate 1080p/720p, small screens, keyboard access, offline loading, OBS public
  views, texture loading and memory cost. Do not load all masters simultaneously.
- Review animation format and skipping/speed controls after the pilot. No GIF,
  sprite sequence, video or runtime animation is delivered by these stills.
