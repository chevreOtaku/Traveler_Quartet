# Illustration and logo catalogue

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-20.
Art direction and generation: Codex. Default content license: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0,
following [LICENSE-CONTENT.txt](../../../../LICENSE-CONTENT.txt).

These are fantasy merchant illustrations and an attributed Ukon adaptation. The catalogue lists
110 full-resolution PNG masters; this repository ships their WebP exports, same size (`web.json`),
and the masters stay outside git. They are ready for visual review (Ukon appearance approved in chat); this copy
does not enable them in the game, add HTTP routes, or validate final crops.

## Presentation contract

- Put names, prices, rents, card text, ownership and results in the interface,
  not inside the illustration.
- User-requested decorative signs ALICE (29) and AGENT N°6 (27) are
  Easter eggs. They do not rename the properties or convey game values.
- The sixteen portal images cover all four fixed routes: 5 to 15, 15 to 25,
  25 to 35 and 35 to 5. Each route has four cosmetic town variants; they do
  not add route choices. Wooden merchant carts are used throughout.
- The sixteen construction images are fixed scenes: one house construction
  and one stall installation per town. Crews are unassigned workers. Player
  appearance, actual actor and building level still require server-driven
  presentation. These stills are not animation frames, transparent layers
  or 3D building models.
- The closed chest illustration does not determine its later die result.
  The Adventurers scene shows a card back without revealing the next event.
- Use the server-filtered card identifier to choose an illustration. A secret
  card must not become identifiable in a public image, URL, alt or DOM attribute.
- The dwarf merchant in these drawings is a recurring card protagonist, not an
  assigned player identity. Male dwarves are bearded; female dwarves are not.
- Masters need banner/card crop review. Rendering at 720p, browser/OBS behavior,
  animations and memory cost have not been validated by this asset transfer.
- Load only the media needed for the current presentation. No remote downloads
  or image generation are required during a game.

The existing three-dimensional merchant models are unchanged.

Wildmark (squares 16, 18 and 19) evokes frontier rest and expedition provisions
from Surviving the Game as a Barbarian, using original characters and architecture.
The west portal uses a stone summoning circle, violet crystals and wooden carts.

Glimmerland (squares 21, 23 and 24) evokes Amagi Brilliant Park with original
mascots and comic attraction maintenance. Park (20) shows a closed jackpot
chest in a restful garden; the live jackpot amount belongs in the interface.

Curtain Call (26, 27 and 29) uses indirect Combatants Will Be Dispatched!
references: two ice/fire-themed background stage directors, a short adult
eccentric scientist at the pub, and the named pub and puppet theater.
The characters and theatrical designs are original.

The north portal uses an angular spatial rift and broken slate monoliths.
Its four variants connect red/yellow town contexts to green/dark blue town
contexts along the same fixed 25-to-35 route.

Wolfcoin (31, 32 and 34) includes a hidden Holo-inspired wolf detail in each
location. Royal Ziggurat (37 and 39) includes a small Gilgamesh-inspired
wealthy visitor in each location. These original scenery characters are
playful references; treasure does not represent player money or a payout.
All 22 district squares now have individual masters. Hidden-detail visibility
at final card/banner sizes remains to be reviewed.

The east portal uses a rectangular stone doorway with open physical door
leaves. All sixteen cosmetic portal variants are now available. Construction
scenes add local houses and stalls without introducing a new building level.

## Chance, World Event and branding

The fourteen Chance and fourteen Event illustrations map to cards.json
identifiers. World Event is the display name for the existing event deck.
Conditional die results, actual targets, amounts, player identity and ownership
remain in server-driven interface text, not encoded as an illustration result.
Keep rope, mirror and a retained curse hidden wherever the view requires it;
never expose their identity through the chosen image, URL, alt or DOM.
The two transparent deck emblems may identify the Chance/Event board squares.
The general logo is a proposal, not a replacement already applied to the front.
These three raster assets have genuine transparency and checked master lettering.
Separate deck backs and a neutral hidden-card mask are now included below. Vector exports and final crops remain to be prepared.

Mana Stone Mining Inc. (12) already has its illustration in this catalogue;
its existing master was hash-verified and retained without duplication.

## Selectable merchant emblems

Five transparent choices: purse, key, compass, quill and scales. Use ONLY
in a reserved property-card header/frame area, as a separate cosmetic owner
stamp. Do not bake them into location images, banners or other illustrations.
The selector, saved choice and owner-seat mapping still require integration.
Render the actual public owner and keep a readable owner label; no stamp on
a free property. The five glyphs are distinguished by shape. Original scene
masters are unchanged. Actual small-size, card layout and OBS checks remain
pending; these assets do not implement a working selector.

The three deck backs are reusable opaque images. The neutral retained-card mask is identical for all hidden cards and carries no deck/content clue. Runtime secrecy and card rendering remain to be verified during integration.

The Ukon mascot is a third-party character adaptation: [creator attribution and license exception](mascot/ATTRIBUTION.md). No avatar model or texture is included.
