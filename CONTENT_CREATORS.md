# Content creator policy

You may **stream** Traveler Quartet and make **videos** of it (YouTube, Twitch and any other platform), **including
monetized content**: ads, subscriptions, donations, sponsorships of your channel.

This permission applies even though the game's licenses are non-commercial. It covers showing and playing the game,
its art and its texts in your content.

Please:

- credit the game ("Traveler Quartet" with a link to this repository) in your video description or stream panel;
- don't present the game as your own, and don't sell the game itself or its assets.

Anything beyond this, such as selling or distributing the game or using it in a paid product, needs the author's
permission. Ask by opening an issue.

The author may update this policy. Content published while a version of this policy was in force stays allowed.
