# Traveler Quartet -- the rules on one page (draft)

*Amounts in brackets are starting values; playtests will adjust them.*

**Goal.** 2 to 4 merchants, **30 rounds**. The richest player at the end wins: cash + value of districts, houses and
stalls. Each player starts with **[750] gold**.

**Who starts.** The first player token passes to the next player at each round: everyone plays once per round, and
each round starts one seat later.

**Your turn.** Roll 2 dice and move. A double: roll again; three doubles in a row: go to the **dungeon**.
When you pass **START**: **+100**, **+10** for each pair you own, plus the income of your stalls.

**Where you land.**
- **Unowned district**: buy it, or decline, and it goes to **auction** (also when you cannot afford it). **Someone
  else's**: pay the rent (nothing if it is mortgaged).
- **Portal**: pay its rent (higher if one player owns all 4). Then you may **jump to the next portal**; nothing happens
  where you land. One jump per lap; a jump across Start pays your salary but counts for two laps.
- **Utility** (water, mana stone): rent = the dice roll times a multiplier.
- **Chance / Event**: draw a card and do what it says.
- **Tax**: pay; the money goes into the **jackpot**. **Park**: you win the jackpot.
- **Go to the Dungeon**: go to the dungeon.

**Auctions.** Everyone bids, starting with you: in turn, each player **raises by [5], [10] or [25]** or **drops out**,
for good. The last one standing buys at the last price; if nobody bids, the district stays free.

**The dungeon.** You stay **2 rounds** (you still collect your rents) and draw an **Adventurer** card each round. Leave
early by paying **[25]**, with an Adventurer card that lets you out, or by rolling a double.

**Building.** You need the **whole town** (its whole color). Houses are built evenly across the town and are in limited
supply. After **3 houses**, a **market stall** (the top level) earns **+20** each time you pass Start.

**Shared stalls.** A rival may build a stall on your district **if you agree**. You each pay half the stall, and you
each earn **+10** when you pass Start; nothing else (the rent stays yours). **One partner per pair.**

**Pairs** (+10 at Start for owning both whole towns): Food (brown + orange) · Money & Leisure (green + red) · Luxury
(light blue + dark blue) · Culture (purple + yellow).

**Short of money.** Sell houses (at half price) or **mortgage** a district. If a partner's stall stands there, refund
their share first. If you cannot pay, you are **out**: you pay what you can, your districts go back to the bank, which
sells them again by **sealed bids** (one secret bid per player, the highest wins), and the game goes on.

**Trading**, on **your** turn only, **3 negotiations per turn** at most (offers and stall requests together). Offer
gold, districts of a town with no buildings, and kept cards (the rope is worth **[50]**, the curse and the mirror
**[100]**). The other player accepts, refuses or makes a counter-offer (**3** counter-offers at most). What each player
receives must be worth **between half and double** what they give. Anything said on the side is free: promises only
bind those who believe them.

**The cards** tell you everything you need. Four to remember:
- **Disasters**: a carousel picks the town, a die picks the damage (even: nothing; 1, 3, 5 = 1, 2, 3 buildings). No
  insurance.
- **Crises** hit a trade; if you own its whole pair, both towns pay, **x1.5**.
- **The curse** is played on another player. Roll a die: **even**, you may keep it; **odd**, play it now.
- **The karma mirror**, kept **secret**: the next curse aimed at you **goes back** to whoever cast it.
